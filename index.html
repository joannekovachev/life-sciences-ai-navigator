About these sources

Life Sciences scenario cards (IDs starting LS-, BIO- and MED-) and the line-of-business overviews were drafted for this Navigator and are illustrative conversation starters, not Microsoft product commitments. Business Applications cards describe first-party Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Copilot Studio capabilities from the Microsoft Business Applications Pre-Built Agents and Capabilities report; preview features may change. Cards marked “Adapted from the healthcare Navigator” are industry-neutral patterns carried over from the healthcare version and should be validated for life sciences. Pattern 5 cards describe Microsoft developer tools, drawn from Microsoft Learn documentation; the custom solution scenarios are illustrative designs. Agentic team architectures are illustrative designs. Commercial and medical use cases should use only approved content and consented data, and promotional content must go through your medical, legal and regulatory review. GxP-relevant uses require validation, data-integrity controls and quality unit approval before production use. All cards should be validated before customer use.

The big idea

AI is not a single product. It is a flow from assistants, to delegation, to agentic teams, to intelligence inside the applications you already use, to custom solutions your own developers build.

1

Five patterns, one evolution

Each pattern changes who does the work, from AI beside one person to AI built into the systems of record, and to AI your own developers build.

    2

    The operating model shifts toward people

    The right pattern moves your people to the high-value work that needs real expertise, and gives AI the low-value work that still needs coordination.

    3

    Agentic teams make it repeatable

    One assist agent is the front door. Specialist agents work across your business systems, and humans stay in the loop for approvals and exceptions.
